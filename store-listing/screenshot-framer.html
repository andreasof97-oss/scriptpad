ScriptPad — Store Screenshot Framer outputs 1280×800 PNG

Turn a plain side-panel screenshot into a polished Chrome Web Store image. Pick your screenshot, add a caption, download. No internet, nothing uploaded — it all happens in your browser.

  1. Open ScriptPad, get the screen you want.
  2. Mac: Cmd+Shift+4, drag over the panel.
  3. Pick that file above.

Repeat for each screen. The store allows up to 5 screenshots — 4–5 is ideal.
