## Detailed Description

📋 **ScriptPad — Your scripts. Always ready. One click away.**

The script assistant built for call center agents, sales reps, and anyone who needs the right words during a live call or meeting. Stop fumbling through Google Docs and sticky notes — ScriptPad puts every script, answer, and next step at your fingertips in a clean side panel, right next to your dialer, CRM, or meeting window.

**🔥 What ScriptPad does:**

✅ **Instant Access** — Opens as a side panel alongside your work. No tab switching.

✅ **One-Click Copy** — Copy any script to your clipboard and paste it mid-call.

✅ **Lightning-Fast Search** — Fuzzy search across every script, title, and tag. Type a few letters, find it instantly.

✅ **Folders, Tags & Pinned Favorites** — Organize by Openers, Objection Handlers, Closers, FAQs. Pin your go-to scripts to the top.

✅ **Decision-Tree Scripts** — Build branching, choose-your-path scripts that guide you through a call step by step, based on what the customer says.

✅ **Knowledge Base** — Keep product info, pricing, and bundles on hand for quick reference.

✅ **AI Assistant** — Ask a question mid-call and get a fast, specific answer drawn from your own knowledge base. (Up to 5 questions/day on the free plan; unlimited with Pro.)

✅ **Notes** — Jot quick notes without leaving the panel.

✅ **Teams** — Managers can build a shared script library and push it to the whole team, so every agent works from the same up-to-date scripts.

✅ **Keyboard Shortcuts** — Ctrl+Shift+S to open, / to search, arrows to navigate. Built for speed.

✅ **Dark & Light Mode** — Easy on the eyes during long shifts.

✅ **Bilingual (EN/ES)** — Full interface in English and Spanish.

✅ **Import/Export** — Back up your scripts as JSON and move between computers.

✅ **Rich Text Editor** — Bold, italic, bullets, and highlights.

**👥 Perfect for:**
• Call center agents (WFH or in-office)
• Telemarketers & cold callers
• Sales Development Reps (SDRs)
• Business owners and anyone prepping for an important sales call or meeting
• Customer service, insurance, and tech support teams
• Anyone who works the phones

**💵 Free plan & Pro:**
ScriptPad is free to start, no credit card required. The free plan includes up to 15 scripts, 5 folders, 15 notes, 20 knowledge-base entries, and 5 AI questions per day. **Pro** unlocks unlimited scripts, folders, notes, and knowledge-base entries, plus unlimited AI Assistant. Team features are available for shared script libraries.

**🛡️ Your data:**
Your scripts and notes are stored locally on your device and work without an account. Optional features — sign-in, Teams, the AI Assistant, and Pro — send only the data needed to run them to our providers (Supabase, Google for AI, and PayPal for payments). We don't sell your data, and we don't use ads or trackers. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
