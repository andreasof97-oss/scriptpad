🔒 ScriptPad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

TL;DR: ScriptPad stores everything locally on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Period.

1. What Data Does ScriptPad Collect?

None. ScriptPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All data you create within ScriptPad — including scripts, folders, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local).

2. What Permissions Does ScriptPad Use?

ScriptPad requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — To save your scripts, folders, and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser.
  • sidePanel — To display ScriptPad as a side panel alongside your current tab, so you can reference scripts while working.

ScriptPad does not request access to your browsing history, tabs, web requests, or any website content.

3. Does ScriptPad Share Data With Third Parties?

No. Since ScriptPad doesn't collect any data, there is nothing to share. We have no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party integrations that receive your data.

4. Does ScriptPad Use Cookies or Tracking?

No. ScriptPad does not use cookies, fingerprinting, or any form of user tracking.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your scripts and settings are stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, which keeps data sandboxed within the extension on your device. This means:

  • Your data stays on your computer
  • Other extensions cannot access your ScriptPad data
  • Uninstalling ScriptPad removes all stored data
  • You can export your data as JSON at any time via Settings → Export

6. Children's Privacy

ScriptPad is designed for professional use by call center agents and sales representatives. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy (for example, if we add optional cloud sync features in the future), we will update this page and note the date of the change. Any features that involve data transmission will always be opt-in with clear disclosure.
