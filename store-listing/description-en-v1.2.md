## Detailed Description

📋 **ScriptPad — Your scripts. Always ready. One click away.**

The script organizer built for people who work the phones. Stop fumbling through Google Docs and sticky notes during calls — ScriptPad puts every script at your fingertips in a clean side panel.

🆕 **NEW in v1.1 — Branching Scripts (Decision Trees):**
Create interactive call flows that guide you step-by-step through complex conversations. Each choice leads to the next step — perfect for qualification flows, troubleshooting guides, and objection handling. Navigate with breadcrumbs and go back anytime.

**🔥 Features:**

✅ **Branching Scripts** — Build interactive decision trees for qualification flows, troubleshooting, and complex call paths. Follow choices step-by-step during live calls.

✅ **Instant Side Panel** — Opens right alongside your dialer or CRM. No tab switching.

✅ **One-Click Copy** — Copy any script to clipboard with a single click. Paste into chat, email, or notes mid-call.

✅ **Lightning-Fast Search** — Fuzzy search across all scripts, titles, and tags. Type a few letters, find it instantly.

✅ **Folders & Tags** — Organize scripts into folders (Openers, Objection Handlers, Closers, FAQs). Add tags for extra organization.

✅ **Keyboard Shortcuts** — Press Ctrl+Shift+S to open. Use / to search, arrow keys to navigate. Built for speed.

✅ **Dark & Light Mode** — Easy on the eyes during long shifts. Switch anytime.

✅ **Bilingual (EN/ES)** — Full interface in English and Spanish. Auto-detects your language.

✅ **Import/Export** — Backup your scripts as JSON. Move between computers. Never lose a script.

✅ **Pin Favorites** — Pin your most-used scripts to the top for instant access.

✅ **Rich Text Editor** — Bold, italic, bullets, highlights. Format scripts exactly how you need them.

**🛡️ Privacy-First:**
All your scripts are stored locally on your device. No accounts required. No data sent to servers. Your scripts stay yours.

**🆓 100% Free:**
ScriptPad is completely free to use with all features included. No hidden paywalls, no trial limits.
